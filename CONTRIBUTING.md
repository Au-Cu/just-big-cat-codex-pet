# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

When contributing animation or direction cells:

1. Preserve the 8-column × 11-row Codex Pet v2 atlas layout.
2. Keep the canvas RGBA with no opaque chroma-key pixels or transparent RGB residue.
3. Preserve the character identity, flat 2D treatment, and thick rounded black outlines.
4. Keep standing legs naturally grounded; do not show forward-facing soles or paw pads.
5. Update the relevant preview and QA files.

By contributing, you agree to license code, configuration, documentation, and scripts under MIT, and your original visual contributions under CC BY 4.0. Do not submit material you do not have the right to share.
